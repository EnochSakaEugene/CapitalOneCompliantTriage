## Statements and due dates
Card issuers must mail or deliver periodic statements at least 21 days before the payment due date, and the due date must be the same day each month.

## Rate increases and changes in terms
Issuers generally must give 45 days' advance written notice before increasing an APR or making other significant changes to account terms, and must tell consumers of their right to reject certain changes. Rate increases on existing balances are restricted, with exceptions such as the end of a disclosed promotional period or a payment more than 60 days late.

## Payment allocation
Amounts paid above the minimum payment must generally be applied first to the balance with the highest APR.

## Credit line decreases and account closures
Issuers may reduce credit lines or close accounts under the cardholder agreement. A credit line reduction or closure based on the consumer's creditworthiness can be an adverse action that requires a notice of the reasons under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and Regulation B.

## Rewards and promotional offers
Rewards, sign-up bonuses and promotional rates must be honored as disclosed. Misleading marketing or changing reward terms without adequate disclosure can raise deceptive or unfair practice concerns.
