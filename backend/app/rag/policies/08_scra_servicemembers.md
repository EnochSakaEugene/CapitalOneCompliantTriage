## Interest rate cap
Under the SCRA, obligations a servicemember incurred before entering military service are capped at 6 percent interest per year during military service, when the servicemember provides written notice and a copy of military orders.

## Handling servicemember complaints
Complaints from servicemembers or their families about interest rates, fees, collections, or account actions during service carry elevated legal risk and should be escalated for specialist review.
