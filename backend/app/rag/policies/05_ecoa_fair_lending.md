## Prohibited discrimination
Creditors may not discriminate against an applicant in any aspect of a credit transaction on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age (provided the applicant can contract), because income derives from public assistance, or because the applicant exercised rights under consumer credit protection laws.

## Adverse action notices
When a creditor takes adverse action, such as denying credit or lowering a credit line, it must notify the applicant, generally within 30 days, and provide the specific principal reasons or disclose the right to request them.

## Handling complaints alleging discrimination
Complaints that allege discriminatory treatment carry high regulatory and legal risk and should be escalated to compliance or fair-lending specialists rather than answered with routine responses.
