## Response timeline
Companies are expected to respond to complaints the CFPB sends them within 15 days. If a company cannot close the complaint within 15 days, it may mark the complaint "in progress" and must provide a final response within 60 days.

## What a good response includes
A company response should address each issue the consumer raised, describe the steps the company took to investigate, state the outcome (for example monetary relief, non-monetary relief, or an explanation), and avoid boilerplate. Consumers can review the company's response and provide feedback.

## Publication
Complaint data, including consumer narratives where the consumer consented, is published in the public Consumer Complaint Database after personal information is removed. Patterns in public complaint data are visible to regulators, press, and researchers.
