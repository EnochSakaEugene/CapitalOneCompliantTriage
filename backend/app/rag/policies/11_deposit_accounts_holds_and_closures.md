## Funds availability (Regulation CC)
Regulation CC sets maximum times banks may hold deposited funds before making them available. Banks may place longer exception holds in specified cases, such as large deposits or reasonable doubt about collectability, but must give the customer notice of the hold and when the funds will be available.

## Account freezes and restrictions
Banks may restrict or freeze accounts to investigate suspected fraud or to meet legal obligations. Long freezes without explanation, or failing to return a consumer's funds, can cause significant harm and have been a focus of CFPB supervision.

## Account closures
When a bank closes a deposit account, it should give the consumer clear information and return any remaining balance promptly. Errors in electronic transfers on deposit accounts follow the Regulation E error resolution process.

## Overdraft and fees
Fees must follow the account agreement and disclosures. Regulators have found certain overdraft and non-sufficient-funds fee practices unfair, such as charging fees on transactions that were authorized against a positive balance.
