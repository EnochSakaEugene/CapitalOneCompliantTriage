## Covered errors
Regulation E covers errors involving electronic fund transfers on consumer accounts such as checking and savings accounts, including unauthorized transfers, incorrect transfer amounts, and omitted transfers on a periodic statement.

## Consumer notice
The consumer should notify the financial institution within 60 days after the institution sent the periodic statement on which the error first appeared.

## Investigation deadlines
The institution must investigate and determine whether an error occurred within 10 business days. It may take up to 45 days if it provisionally credits the consumer's account within the 10 business days. It must report results to the consumer and correct any error promptly.

## Liability for unauthorized transfers
Consumer liability for unauthorized electronic transfers depends on how quickly the consumer reports the loss or theft of an access device, rising the longer the consumer waits.
