## Scope
The FDCPA and Regulation F govern third-party debt collectors. They prohibit harassment, false or misleading representations, and unfair practices in collecting debts.

## Validation and disputes
Collectors must provide validation information about a debt. If a consumer disputes the debt in writing within the validation period, the collector must stop collection until it provides verification.

## Communications
Collectors may not call at unusual or inconvenient times, contact consumers at work when they know it is prohibited, or communicate with third parties about the debt except as permitted.
