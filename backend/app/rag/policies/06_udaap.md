## Standard
The Consumer Financial Protection Act prohibits unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices in connection with consumer financial products. An act may be unfair if it causes substantial injury consumers cannot reasonably avoid and that is not outweighed by benefits. It may be deceptive if it misleads a reasonable consumer about a material fact.

## Common complaint signals
Repeated complaints about unexpected fees, misleading promotional rates, interest charged contrary to disclosures, or difficulty closing accounts can indicate a systemic practice that should be reviewed for UDAAP risk.

## Systemic issues
When many consumers report the same problem, a company should look for root causes, remediate affected consumers, and document corrective action.
