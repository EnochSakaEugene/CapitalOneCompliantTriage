## Dispute investigations
When a consumer disputes the accuracy or completeness of information in their credit file, the consumer reporting agency must conduct a reasonable reinvestigation, generally within 30 days, and notify the furnisher of the information.

## Furnisher duties
A company that furnishes information to credit bureaus, such as a card issuer or bank, must investigate disputes it receives, review relevant information, report results, and correct or delete information found to be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable.

## Identity theft
Consumers who are victims of identity theft can request that fraudulent information be blocked from their credit file and can place fraud alerts.
