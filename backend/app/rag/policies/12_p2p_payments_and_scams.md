## Unauthorized transfers
Regulation E protects consumers from unauthorized electronic fund transfers, including peer-to-peer payments started by someone else without the consumer's permission, such as after an account takeover. These follow the Regulation E notice, investigation and liability rules.

## Payments the consumer was tricked into sending
When a consumer is deceived into sending a payment themselves, the transfer has generally been treated as authorized under Regulation E, so the error resolution rules may not require a refund. Many institutions still review scam claims, offer goodwill relief, and share fraud-prevention guidance.

## Handling scam and fraud complaints
Fraud and scam complaints should be routed to fraud specialists quickly, with clear communication about the investigation, timelines and outcome.
