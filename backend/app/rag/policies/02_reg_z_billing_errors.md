## Covered billing errors
Billing errors on open-end credit (such as credit cards) include charges the consumer did not make or authorize, charges for goods or services not accepted or not delivered as agreed, the creditor's failure to credit a payment or return, computational errors, and charges for which the consumer requests documentation.

## Consumer notice
The consumer must send written notice of the billing error so that the creditor receives it within 60 days after the creditor sent the first statement showing the error.

## Creditor obligations and deadlines
The creditor must acknowledge the notice in writing within 30 days of receiving it, unless it resolves the dispute sooner. It must resolve the dispute within two complete billing cycles, and in no case later than 90 days. While the dispute is pending, the creditor may not try to collect the disputed amount or report it as delinquent.

## Unauthorized use
Under the Truth in Lending Act, a cardholder's liability for unauthorized use of a credit card is capped at 50 dollars, and many issuers offer zero-liability policies.
